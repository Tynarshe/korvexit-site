Switching IT providers

  1. Discovery

    Identify the systems people need to do their jobs and the problems that interrupt them.

  2. Handover

    Agree who owns each account, how suppliers will transfer access and how staff will ask for help.

  3. First priorities

    Review urgent access issues, backup coverage and devices that need attention.

  4. Review

    Check what has improved and agree the next set of priorities.
